About

A developer's precision, an analyst's curiosity.

I'm a web developer and data analytics enthusiast, currently working full-time while having completed my Master's in Computer Science with a specialization in Data Analytics. I sit at the intersection of building the systems that generate data and interpreting what that data is trying to say.

On the development side, that means full-stack Laravel and PHP applications with clean, maintainable structure. I care about the same thing in both: does this actually work, and does it actually help someone decide something.
